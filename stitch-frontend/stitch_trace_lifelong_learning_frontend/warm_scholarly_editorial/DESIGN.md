## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intentional, scholarly, and human-centered digital landscape for a lifelong learning ecosystem. Rejecting the cold, hyper-saturated trends of corporate software and electric SaaS interfaces, the visual direction embraces a quiet academic sophistication—drawing deep inspiration from curated archival journals, timeless editorial broadsheets, and tactile warm paper materials.

The audience consists of lifelong researchers, educators, scholars, and institutional learners who spend sustained periods reading, annotating, and mapping knowledge. The visual atmosphere evokes quiet focus, intellectual authority, and enduring warmth.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Minimalism
- **Open Asymmetric Architecture:** An expansive layout featuring generous margins and deliberate asymmetry rather than rigid, boxed-in dashboard tiles.
- **Tactile Paper Materiality:** Natural layering evoking heavy archival rag paper, vellum sheets, and bookbinder boards rather than synthetic glass or plastic finishes.
- **Restrained Chromatic Structure:** An earthbound, warm parchment foundation balanced by rich deep brown-charcoal ink and focused terracotta accents for purposeful navigational orientation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unbroken continuum of warm organic tones, deliberately avoiding cold synthetic grays and stark sterile digital whites.

### Primary Roles
- **Primary (`#BF9270` - Terracotta):** The definitive active pigment. Applied deliberately to primary call-to-actions, active filter states, focused interactive borders, and key telemetry milestones.
- **Secondary (`#E3B7A0` - Visual Trace):** Supporting accent used for subtle progress indicators, passive indicators, interactive hover fills, and marginal notations.
- **Tertiary (`#EDCDBB` - Muted Sand):** Transitional structural surface tint for section breaks, secondary panels, metadata blocks, and quiet content dividers.
- **Neutral (`#1A1412` - Warm Charcoal):** The anchoring ink tone for high-authority headings, body reading text, and the foundational background tone of the immersive editorial footer.

### Chromatic Harmony & Surface Context
- The root canvas is anchored in `#FFEDDB` (Warm Cream / Laid Paper), setting a gentle eye-level luminosity.
- Primary reading vessels, long-form editorial essays, and critical document planes emerge cleanly via `#FFFFFF`.
- High-contrast anchoring is preserved across the footer and thematic deep-reading modes using `#1A1412` and `#261D1A` populated with `#FFEDDB` typography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dialogue between historical intellectual craft and modern pragmatic interface design.

### Font Roles
- **Editorial Headlines & Display:** `EB Garamond` commands all primary hero titles, chapter openings, section headlines, and pull quotes. It provides a literary, academic gravitas that dignifies the learning materials.
- **Interface, Body, & Metadata:** `Manrope` delivers crystalline geometric legibility across long-form reading sequences, interactive menus, button actions, metadata labels, and metric trackers.
- **Multilingual Tamil System:** Bilingual typography preserves identical weight and presence through complementary typefaces: Noto Serif Tamil reflects the optical texture and proportion of the Latin serif titles, while Noto Sans Tamil provides consistent structure alongside Manrope for body text and navigation.
- **Numerical Telemetry:** All curricular milestones, index numbers, and data points should enforce tabular numerals (`tnum`) in Manrope to ensure proportional stability across responsive tables and progress bars.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is informed by classic editorial broadsheets and open academic volumes, avoiding dense, cramped grid layouts in favor of generous, readable negative space.

### Grid Architecture & Form Factors
- **Max Content Constellation:** 1200px maximum inner content width, centered with ample fluid breathing room on ultra-wide viewports.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetric 12-column grid featuring `margin-lg` (4rem) and `gutter-lg` (2.5rem). The content layout frequently leans into an 8-column primary narrative column balanced by a 4-column column reserved for marginalia, study notes, and research citations.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin` (2rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). Secondary notes collapse gracefully into collapsible drawer blocks or inline annotations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm` (1.25rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Sections reflow strictly into an uninterrupted vertical editorial scroll.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed entirely through layered tonal planes and crisp, warm low-contrast outlines rather than synthetic drop-shadows or skeuomorphic bevels.

### Layering Philosophy
1. **Parchment Substrate:** `#FFEDDB` forms the base viewport canvas.
2. **Intermediate Panels:** `#EDCDBB` creates natural transitions for secondary sidebars, study modules, and contextual tool trays.
3. **Elevated Reading Sheets:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces rest softly upon the warm ground, bounded strictly by hairline 1px rules (`#DFC1B0`).
4. **Interactive Overlays & Modals:** Elevated dialogue leaves use `#FFFFFF` defined by a 1px `#DFC1B0` border and an ambient, low-contrast warm umber shadow: `0 8px 30px -6px rgba(26, 20, 18, 0.08)`.
5. **Anchoring Foundation:** The footer commands the base of the page with `#1A1412` and `#261D1A`, creating a resolute visual conclusion to long-form reading journeys.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary mirrors physical printed volumes, bound library editions, and index cards with restrained, deliberate softening.

- **Panels, Content Sheets, & Inputs:** Calibrated consistently to an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius. This prevents the interface from feeling either clinical or excessively bubbly.
- **Status Pills, Curricular Tags, & Badges:** Use full pill radii (`9999px`) to distinguish discrete interactive tags from structural text blocks.
- **Controls & Checkboxes:** Softened with a slight 4px (`0.25rem`) radius for precise visual stability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid terracotta fill (`#BF9270`) with crisp ivory-cream text (`#FFEDDB`). Uses `label-md` in Manrope, 8px border radius, and `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover state deepens smoothly to `#A87E5E`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid border in `#DFC1B0` and text in `#1A1412`. On hover, the background shifts to `#FFEDDB`.
- **Ghost / Text Action:** No background or border. Rendered in `#BF9270` with an animated baseline underline transition.

### Cards & Reading Sheets
- Minimalist, non-boxed aesthetic.
- Surfaces leverage `#FFFFFF` surrounded by a clean 1px border in `#DFC1B0` with an 8px radius.
- Padding follows `space-lg` (1.5rem) internally. Cards avoid heavy shadows, drawing contrast directly from the `#FFEDDB` canvas beneath them.

### Input Fields & Search Wells
- Clean `#FFFFFF` background framed with a 1px `#DFC1B0` border and an 8px radius.
- Text input renders in `#1A1412` with placeholder text set in muted umber `#5F524B`.
- Focused state applies a 1.5px border in terracotta `#BF9270` with zero diffuse glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- 18x18px containers with a 1px solid `#DFC1B0` boundary and 4px radius (or circle for radio).
- Active state transitions to `#BF9270` fill displaying an ivory check or dot icon.

### Chips, Progress Traces & Badges
- **Curricular Chips:** Muted background in `#EDCDBB` with `#1A1412` text in `label-sm`. Fully pill-shaped.
- **Progress Traces:** Continuous track in `#EDCDBB` filled dynamically with `#E3B7A0` (subtle trace) or `#BF9270` (active completion milestone).

### Archival Marginalia & Editorial Notes
- Specialized contextual reading column components. Left-bordered with `2px solid #BF9270`.
- Styled using `editorial-quote` in EB Garamond or `body-sm` in `#5F524B`.

### Dark Warm-Brown Footer
- Deep anchoring background in `#1A1412` and `#261D1A`.
- Headings set in EB Garamond with body copy and links set in `#FFEDDB` (warm cream) with 70% opacity for secondary links.
